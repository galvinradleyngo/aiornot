Game Rules & Facilitation Guide

Game Objective & Flow

"To AI or Not to AI?" is a case-method card game designed to build shared vocabulary around the risks and opportunities of using AI in a given role.

  1. Dealing: 26 Risk & Opportunity cards are distributed across players as their watch area.
  2. Scenario Reveal: Host reveals a Use Case scenario (1 of 18).
  3. Scanning & Flagging: Players inspect their dealt hand and flag relevant Risk/Opportunity cards.
  4. Top Picks Vote: Everyone votes for their top 2 Risks and top 2 Opportunities; results are consolidated on the host screen.
  5. Debate & Consensus: Group discusses the top-voted Risks and Opportunities that apply.
  6. Decision: Commit to TO AI, NOT TO AI, or TO AI — WITH GUARDRAILS (plus guardrail text).
